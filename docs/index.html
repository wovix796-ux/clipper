Questions

Is Clipper really free?

Yes. Clipper is free and open source under the MIT license. No ads, no subscription, no account.

Does Clipper record my friends on Discord?

Yes. Clipper records your PC audio, which includes everyone you hear on the call, and mixes in your own microphone.

How do I save the last 30 seconds on my PC?

Install Clipper and press Alt+R. The last 30 seconds are saved to Videos\Clips. You can change the key and the length (15 seconds to 5 minutes) in the settings.

Can Clipper take screenshots?

Yes. Press Alt+F1 and Clipper saves a PNG of your main screen to Videos\Clips. A small preview pops up in the top right without pulling you out of the game, and it never shows up in your clips. Click the preview to open the screenshot in Clipper. You can change the key in the settings.

Is Clipper made by Discord?

No. Clipper is an independent project and is not affiliated with Discord. It works with any game or app, with or without Discord.

Windows says the app is unrecognized. Is it safe?

Windows SmartScreen warns about apps that aren't signed with a paid certificate. Click "More info" and "Run anyway". The full source code is on GitHub.
